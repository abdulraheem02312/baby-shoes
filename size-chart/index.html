How to measure

Measure your foot in 3 steps

  1. 1

    Stand on paper

    Put a blank paper on the floor against a wall. Stand on it with your heel touching the wall.

  2. 2

    Mark your longest toe

    Ask someone to draw a line at the tip of your longest toe. Do it in the evening, when feet are a little bigger.

  3. 3

    Measure and match

    Measure from the wall to the line in centimetres, then find that number in the chart below.

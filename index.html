FAQs

Questions? We have answers

Everything you need to know before you order.

How do I place an order?

Open the Shop, tap "See More" on the pair you like, choose your size and tap "Order on WhatsApp". Send us your address and we will confirm your order.

Do you deliver all over Pakistan?

Yes. We deliver to every city in Pakistan, right to your door.

How do I find my size?

Check our size chart and measure your foot. Still not sure? Message us on WhatsApp with your usual size and we will help you pick the right fit.

A shoe says "Out of stock". What can I do?

Open it and tap "Ask when it's back". It sends us a WhatsApp message and we will let you know as soon as it is in stock again.

Do you have baby shoes?

Yes. In the shop, turn on the "Baby sizes" switch to see styles in baby size, then tell us the baby's age on WhatsApp.

Can I visit the shop?

Of course. You will find us at Kafar Market, Churi Bazar, Vehari. See the map.
